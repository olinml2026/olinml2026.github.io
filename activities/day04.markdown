# Design Sprint

{% capture content %}
Form a group of four.  Introduce yourselves.  You can work in the classroom or out in the hall (or in a different 
classroom).  Please make sure that we know where you are.

You will be exploring the potential of machine learning (and optionally wearables) to positively impact some aspect 
of employment for people how are blind.  For example, you  might create a piece of technology to help with 
applying to jobs, job training, job retention, or upskilling.

We want to start by recognizing that you all are far from experts on issues facing people who are blind.  That said, 
we want you to feel comfortable to let yourself be creative, ask questions, and dream.  Brian will be here to give 
feedback and help guide you.  Brian can speak from his own experience, but he has interacted with thousands of blind 
people over the years in his personal and professional capacity.  As such, he is a great person to get general 
direction and feedback from.

Here are some activities:
* Make a map of the employment process.  This could include education, training, finding jobs, interviewing, 
  performing jobs, getting promoted, etc.
* Based on what you heard from Brian (or your own research), what are some parts of this process that might be pain 
  points for people who are blind.
* Choose a part of the process to focus on.  How would you learn more about this opportunity space?  Make a list of 
  potential activities to do or resources to consult.  Consider running it by Brian.
* For the part of the process you chose to focus on, come up with some ways that machine learning could potentially 
  improve the experience of people who are blind.
* You may repeat this exercise to explore other parts of the employment process.  Also, consider asking Brian for 
  feedback.
