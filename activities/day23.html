      Environmental Impacts of Large Language Models

      Exercise 1

      The environmental impact of large language models is one of the biggest concerns around the technology. Here are a few resources you can check out to learn more.

      As you go, you engage with these resources try to think critically about what you are reading.

      • Who is writing the article?
      • How are they measuring environmental impact? Is it accounting for inference-only or inference with amortized training costs?

      • What costs might be left out of their analysis?

      • A measurement of Google Gemini’s energy usage per query
      • The environmental impact of using a particular GPU in a particular datacenter
      • Some models on Hugging Face actually show training CO2 emissions. The JobBERTv3 model uses comparatively little CO2 (0.717 kg) whereas the BLOOM model uses a lot (24,700 kg).
      • Gen AI’s Environmental Ledger: A Closer Look at the Carbon Footprint of ChatGPT
      • Find some articles or estimates of your own. What is the environmental impact a particular AI model? Perhaps focus on models that may be either more or less efficient.

      We’ll do a quick report out on your findings.
